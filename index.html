Sushant Chadha

CFD, fluid–structure interaction and simulation automation for the Texas A&M SAE Aero Design team.

I build simulation workflows that answer real design questions for a competition aircraft team: coupling Ansys and STAR-CCM+ to see how a flexible wing changes its own aerodynamics, modeling propeller wash to settle a motor-placement debate, and benchmarking GPU solvers on Texas A&M's HPRC clusters to decide which software version the whole team should move to.

Each study below is written up the same way — Overview → Setup → Results → Challenges & Conclusion — so you can skim the takeaways or dig into the numbers.

    About

    I'm an engineering student at Texas A&M University and a member of the SAE Aero Design team, where I lead the team's simulation tooling. My interests sit at the intersection of aerodynamics, structures and computing: making high-fidelity analysis fast and repeatable enough that the team can actually use it during a design cycle.

    Beyond the studies on this site, I maintain the team's STAR-CCM+ base simulations, help teammates get set up on the HPRC clusters, and am currently working on validating our CFD against flight data from an onboard inertial measurement unit.
